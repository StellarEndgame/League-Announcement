🏛 About The League

TC195CL brings chess clubs together through friendly and competitive Multi-Club Arenas, creating a league where clubs can compete, represent their communities, and fight for the top of the standings.

🌍 Compete.   •   🤝 Represent.   •   🏆 Conquer.


🏆 League Format

  • ⚡ 3+2 Blitz — 48h Multi-Club Arena
  • 📅 Every Saturday — 13:00 UTC (6:30 PM IST)
  • 🏆 Monthly seasons
  • 📊 Results count toward league standings
  • 🥇 Top clubs and players receive trophies
